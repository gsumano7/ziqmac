About

Ziq Mac is a photographer based in Portland, Oregon, working in portraits and landscape across the Pacific Northwest.

Approach

Available light, long days, and as few people on set as the job allows. Most of the landscape work is shot within a few hours of home; the portrait work happens in a small daylight studio in Southeast Portland.

Commissions run from single-day editorial shoots to multi-week documentary projects. Prints of the personal work are available on request.
