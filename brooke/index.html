Six names for what comes after Brooke’s Beauties

You said you’ve always struggled to land on a name. Here are six directions built around what you actually do — lashes, makeup, facials, skincare, and reiki — each with a look, a tagline, and the thinking behind it. React to any of them, mix and match, or tell me what’s close.

What the name has to hold

You’re a Master Esthetician, but reiki is what makes you different — almost no lash studio offers energy work. The strongest names below lean into that overlap: beauty on the outside, calm on the inside.

  • Lash extensions
  • Makeup
  • Facials
  • Skincare
  • Reiki
Ziq’s pick

Concept 01

Aura & Lash

Beauty you can see. Energy you can feel.

“Aura” is the bridge between your two worlds — it’s literally a reiki word, but everyone also uses it to mean glow. Paired with “Lash,” it says exactly what you do and exactly what makes you different, in three syllables. Easy to say, easy to search, and the ampersand gives the logo a built-in mark.

Concept 02

The Brook

Still waters. Radiant skin.

Your name literally means a small, clear stream — calm, natural, always moving. This keeps “you” in the business without the alliteration you’ve outgrown. It reads like a serene studio you step into, and it ages beautifully if you ever expand beyond solo work.

Concept 03

Glow & Flow

Facials for the glow. Reiki for the flow.

The most playful option. “Glow” is skincare, “flow” is energy work — the rhyme makes it sticky and it practically writes its own Instagram captions. Best fit if your brand voice is warm and fun rather than high-end spa.

Concept 04

Halo Studio

Framed in light.

Lashes frame the eyes the way a halo frames light — and “halo” quietly nods to auras and energy too. One soft word, instantly premium, and the circle motif gives you an obvious logo, favicon, and sticker.

Concept 05

Lumière by Brooke

Light, in every sense.

French for “light” — the most elevated, spa-luxury direction here. “by Brooke” keeps it personal and makes clear there’s one artist behind it. Choose this one if you want to position toward higher-ticket facials and bridal makeup.

Concept 06

Skin & Soul

Esthetics with energy work.

The most literal of the six: it names both halves of the business and nothing else. Clear, honest, and easy to explain in one breath — the tradeoff is that it’s a common pairing, so it may be harder to own the handle and the trademark.

Before you fall in love with one

Whichever direction feels right: check that the Instagram handle is free, search your state’s business registry for conflicts, and say it out loud like you’re answering the phone. If it survives all three, it’s a real name. Then I’ll build out the full logo and design concept around the winner.
