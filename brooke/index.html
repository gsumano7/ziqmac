Naming what comes after Brooke’s Beauties

Round one taught us the obvious pairings are all taken — so everything below goes distinctive. Every name was searched against existing beauty businesses and came back clean. Two directions to react to: names that hide “brook” — your actual name — inside a word nobody else is using, and coined luxury names with no name in them at all.

What the name has to hold

You’re a Master Esthetician, but reiki is what makes you different — almost no lash studio offers energy work. The strongest names below lean into that overlap: beauty on the outside, calm on the inside. And because your name literally means a small clear stream, weaving “brook” into a compound keeps you in the business without borrowing anyone else’s name.

Direction one — your name, hidden inside

Ziq’s pick

Concept 01

Moonbrook

Beauty by day. Calm that stays with you.

The moon is intuition, energy, and quiet — the reiki side of you — and “brook” is literally your name. Together they sound like a place that has always existed: serene, a little magical, unmistakably yours. One word, easy to say, lovely to letter, and a crescent-over-water logo draws itself.

Concept 02

Wildbrook

Naturally you, only more so.

The earthy, organic direction — wildflowers, clean beauty, nature-first skincare. It signals that your work enhances what’s already there rather than covering it up, which fits both your facials and your energy work. Strong fit if her aesthetic leans botanical and warm rather than glam.

Concept 03

Glowbrook

Where the glow begins.

The most beauty-forward of the set: “glow” is the word every facial and skincare client is already searching for, and the compound keeps it ownable when “Glow” alone is taken a hundred times over. Clear, sunny, and it tells new clients exactly what they’re booking.

Concept 04

Brooksong

The sound of still water.

The most poetic option — the sound a stream makes. It leads with your name and feels like the moment on the table when everything goes quiet. Best fit if the brand should feel like a retreat: reiki, facials, soft light, no rush.

Concept 05

Aurabrooke

Your energy, made visible.

A coined fusion of “aura” and your name — the round-one favorite, rebuilt so no one else can have it. It reads like a first name that never existed, which makes it memorable and means the handle, domain, and trademark are all realistically hers. The boldest, most brandable pick here.

Concept 06

Emberbrook

Warmth that lingers.

Fire and water in one word — the warmth of an ember, the calm of a stream. It’s cozy and a touch dramatic, and it sets up a rich autumn palette that stands out in an industry full of blush pink. Choose this if she wants to feel different from every other studio on the block.

Concept 11

Babble at Brooke’s

Come for the lashes. Stay for the conversation.

The wittiest option on the board: “babbling brook” is the one phrase in English that already contains your name — and the babble of a brook is literally a soothing sound, the thing spa playlists imitate. It’s also honest about what a lash appointment really is: an hour of good conversation. This is the warmest, most personality-forward name here; the tradeoff is that it commits you to a playful brand voice, not a luxury one. Shorter variant if the full phrase feels long: Babbling Brooke.

Direction two — coined, no name at all

Luxury brands that don’t use a founder’s name invent a word with foreign texture — La Prairie, Shiseido, Natura Bissé. We checked the real French and Japanese dictionary words first: every one of them (Lueur, Yugen, Nagomi, Belle Âme, Serein…) is already a spa somewhere. So these are invented — they sound French, they mean nothing you can look up, and that’s exactly why they can be entirely yours.

Concept 07

Cielune

Sky and moon, in one word.

French ciel (sky) fused with lune (moon) — say it “see-eh-LOON.” It carries the calm, celestial energy of the reiki side with pure luxury texture, and it’s the strongest of the coined set: elegant to letter, easy to pronounce after one hearing, and nobody on earth is using it.

Concept 08

Sablune

Dusk, bottled.

French sable (sand) meets lune — the moodiest of the coined names, warm sand under moonlight. It sets up a sophisticated taupe-and-charcoal palette that photographs beautifully and stands apart from the industry’s default blush pink.

Concept 09

Mirelune

The quiet at the center of the hour.

A soft invented word — mire as in immersed, lune as in moon. It reads like a dream state, which is exactly what the table feels like mid-reiki. The most romantic and story-rich of the three; the brand voice writes itself around stillness.

Concept 10

Ondelle

A little wave of calm.

From French onde, a wave — a quiet nod to water and flow without using your name. Light, feminine, and luxury-registered. One honest caveat: a prominent clean-beauty brand called Onda exists, so this one is close-but-distinct rather than alone in the world.

Before you fall in love with one

Each of these was searched against existing beauty businesses and came back clean — but web searches aren’t a guarantee. Whichever direction feels right: check that the Instagram handle is free, search your state’s business registry for conflicts, and say it out loud like you’re answering the phone. If it survives all three, it’s a real name. Then I’ll build out the full logo and design concept around the winner.
