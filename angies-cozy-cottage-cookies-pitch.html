Why this palette, for these cookies

Cloud Dancer is a warm off-white, not a stark one — it photographs like paper rather than like a screen, so your cookies stay the brightest thing in the frame. Wild Strawberry does the opposite job: it's the color that stops a thumb mid-scroll. One is the room, the other is the doorbell.

Your brown sits between them and holds everything together. Because the logo never changes color, every piece here puts it on Cloud Dancer — a printed label on a pink bag, a paper panel on a pink tag. That's the one rule the whole system runs on, and it's what makes a Paw Patrol set and a christening set look like they came from the same kitchen.
