Why this matters for a cookie business

Most of your orders arrive by DM, get photographed on a countertop, and leave in a box. Those three moments — the feed, the photo, the box — are the whole brand experience. Right now the logo carries all of it alone.

A defined palette makes every set you post look like it came from the same kitchen, even when the cookies themselves are Paw Patrol one week and a christening the next. Packaging turns a delivery into something the customer photographs and tags. That's how a 168-follower account becomes a booked-out one.
