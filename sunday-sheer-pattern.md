# Sunday Sheer

A top-down raglan mesh pullover · One Size

---

## Materials

- **Yarn:** Loops & Threads Everyday Cotton (#4 medium weight; 100% cotton; 120 yd / 109 m per 2.5 oz / 70.9 g skein) — 6 skeins (approx. 720 yd / 654 m)
- **Needles:**
  - US 6 / 4 mm circular needles, for the body and sleeves
  - US 2.5 / 3 mm circular needles, for the ribbing
- **Notions:** 5 mm (US H-8) crochet hook for binding off; 4 stitch markers (1 unique for beginning of round); waste yarn or stitch holders; tapestry needle

## Gauge

13 sts × 18 rounds = 4" × 4" (10 × 10 cm) in mesh pattern on 4 mm needles

## Size

One size. Finished chest: approx. 22" measured flat across the chest. The mesh fabric is open and stretchy, so the fit is forgiving.

## Abbreviations

- **BOR** — beginning of round
- **CO** — cast on
- **DS** — double stitch (German short rows)
- **k** — knit
- **k1tbl** — knit 1 through the back loop
- **k2tog** — knit 2 together
- **p** — purl
- **pm** — place marker
- **sm** — slip marker
- **skp** — slip 1 knitwise, knit 1, pass the slipped stitch over
- **s2kp** — slip 2 together knitwise, knit 1, pass both slipped stitches over
- **yo2** — yarn over twice (double yarn over)

## Stitch patterns

**Twisted 1×1 rib (in the round):** (K1tbl, p1) around. All ribbing in this pattern is worked with the knit stitches through the back loop so the ribbing holds its shape.

**Mesh repeat:** skp, yo2, s2kp, yo2.

## Notes

- The sweater is worked seamlessly from the top down: a ribbed neckband, then a raglan yoke shaped with increases at each of the four section markers, with German short rows across the back to raise the back neck. The body and sleeves are then separated and each finished in the round.
- Cast on using the German twisted long-tail cast on.
- Each increase round adds 24 sts: 3 sts at the beginning and 3 sts at the end of each of the four sections.
- All bind-offs are worked with the 5 mm crochet hook to keep the edges stretchy.

---

## Pattern

### Neckband

With 3 mm needles, CO 76 sts using the German twisted long-tail cast on. Join to work in the round, being careful not to twist, and pm for BOR.

Work twisted 1×1 rib for 6 rounds, or approx. ¾".

### Yoke

Switch to 4 mm needles. The round is divided into four sections: first sleeve, front, second sleeve, back.

**Round 1 (set-up):** [Mesh repeat] 4 times, k2tog, pm; [mesh repeat] 8 times, pm; [mesh repeat] 4 times, pm; [mesh repeat] 8 times to end of round.

**Round 2:** \*K1, (p1, k2) to marker, sm; repeat from \* to end of round.

**Round 3 (increase round):** \*Into the first stitch of the section, work (slip 1 knitwise, yo2, k1) all in the same stitch; continue in mesh pattern as for Round 1 to the last stitch before the marker; into that last stitch, work (k2tog, yo2, then knit 1 more into the same stitch); sm; repeat from \* to end of round — 24 sts increased (6 per section).

### Back-neck short rows (German short rows)

When you reach the back (last) section, shape the back neck as follows:

1. Work in pattern across the back section until 3 sts remain before the BOR marker. Turn.
2. **Short row (WS):** Make a DS, then work back across the back section in pattern as it faces you (p1, k1, p1, p1 repeating) until 3 sts remain before the marker at the other end of the back section. Turn.
3. **Short row (RS):** Make a DS, then work in pattern all the way back to the BOR marker, working each DS as a single stitch when you come to it.

Resume working in the round.

### Yoke, continued

Continue repeating Rounds 1 and 2, working an increase round (as Round 3) every 10th round, until 6 more increase rounds have been worked after Round 3 — 7 increase rounds total, 244 sts on the needles: 55 sts for each sleeve and 67 sts each for the front and back.

Work 10 more rounds even in pattern.

### Separating the body and sleeves

Place the first 55 sleeve sts on waste yarn. Using the cable cast on, CO 6 sts. Work in pattern across the 67 front sts to the marker. Place the next 55 sleeve sts on a second length of waste yarn. Cable CO 6 sts. Work in pattern across the 67 back sts — 146 body sts.

### Body

K3 (to the middle of the underarm cast-on sts) and pm — this is your new BOR. Begin the mesh pattern as for Round 1 and work around until you reach the middle of the cast-on sts at the other underarm; pm. Continue in pattern (Rounds 1 and 2) in the round until the body measures 11" from the underarm separation, or your desired length.

**Next round:** (K1, p1) to end of round.

Bind off all sts on the following round using the 5 mm crochet hook.

### Sleeves (make 2)

Return the 55 held sts of one sleeve to the 4 mm needles. Beginning at the middle of the underarm cast-on sts, pick up and knit 6 sts across the underarm — 61 sts. Join to work in the round; the round begins at the middle of the underarm.

Work in mesh pattern (Rounds 1 and 2) until the sleeve measures 18" from the underarm, or your desired length.

Switch to 3 mm needles and work twisted 1×1 rib for 6 rounds. Bind off using the 5 mm crochet hook.

### Finishing

Weave in all ends, using the underarm tails to close any small gaps where the sleeves meet the body. Gently wet block if desired.
